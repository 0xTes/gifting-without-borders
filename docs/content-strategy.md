# Content Strategy

## Editorial principles

- Dignity over pity.
- Evidence over exaggeration.
- People over statistics.
- Context around impact.
- Truthful incomplete states over invented completeness.
- International-first, humanitarian language rather than religious positioning.
- A concise editorial tone: show, then tell.

The platform should communicate care, accountability, and practical support without performative charity language, pressure tactics, or generic marketing personas.

## Content categories

| Category | Purpose | Publication boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Organizational profile | Explain purpose, Mission, Vision, Values, and leadership. | Requires approved organizational wording and verified people. |
| Projects | Describe the purpose and context of specific work. | Use only approved facts, status, imagery, and reporting context. |
| Impact | Explain evidence and responsibly report results. | Publish metrics only with a source, scope, period, and methodology. |
| Stories | Add human context to the work. | Requires informed consent, context, display preference, and imagery permission. |
| Transparency/reporting | Make reports and supporting material findable. | Link only approved, current, publicly shareable materials. |
| Donation information | Explain how support can be given safely. | Display financial instructions and payment links only after independent verification. |

## Factual integrity

Every public claim needs an accountable source and approval path. Do not infer identities, locations, needs, outcomes, or beneficiary counts from imagery. Do not rewrite historical content into stronger current claims. If current evidence is unavailable, retain a useful content-ready state and say only what the record establishes.

Project imagery must have known organizational provenance and appropriate publication context. Stories must never make a person’s hardship the primary visual or rhetorical device. Content owners should review material for accuracy, consent, dignity, and currency before publication.
