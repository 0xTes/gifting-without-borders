# Content Evidence Matrix

Audit date: 2026-08-20. Scope: organization-published material at `https://giftingwithoutborder.org/`, owner-supplied material, and recovered assets listed in `docs/asset-inventory.md`.

## Evidence classes

- **Class A — authoritative:** current, dated organization-published reports, governance material, verified payment instructions, or approved stories with the information needed for safe publication.
- **Class B — contextual:** organization-published or owner-supplied material that supports a public description, image placement, or historic context, but does not independently establish a current fact, outcome, date, location, identity, consent, or methodology.
- **Class C — not safe to reuse:** incomplete, unsupported, third-party, or otherwise insufficient material.

## Publication boundaries

- Public UI uses natural organization-facing language; source and evidence limits remain in repository documentation.
- Image alternative text describes observable content only. It does not assign roles, relationships, locations, dates, or results.
- Primary bank-transfer details are intentionally limited to the exact values shown in the owner-supplied screenshot and are the only active giving route.
- Future updates need either a newly approved exact source or independently verified reporting material; they must not be inferred from existing contextual sources.
