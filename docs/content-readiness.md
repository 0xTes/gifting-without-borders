# Content Readiness

This is the operational checklist for moving from a truthful content-ready platform to an authoritative public nonprofit website. Each input needs a named organizational owner and approval before publication.

| Domain | Required inputs | Readiness classification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Organization | Formal description, approved Mission, Vision, Values, leadership names, titles, biographies, and any registration details intended for publication. | Required before public launch |
| Contact and location | Office address, public email, public phone, map/location approval, and operating hours if relevant. | Required before public launch |
| Projects | Official title, status, dates, location, objective, activities, outcomes, verified metrics, approved photography, and consent/context where available. | Required before project-detail activation or expansion |
| Impact | Reporting period, methodology, verified totals, evidence source, and approval owner. | Required before impact-reporting activation |
| Stories | Consent, name/display preference, role/context, approved quote or story, and imagery permission. | Required before story publication |
| Transparency | Annual Report, Financial Overview, Impact Summary, and approved report or video URLs. | Required before transparency-material activation |
| Donations | Independently verified Bank Transfer details, Paystack link, Flutterwave link, OPay details, PalmPay details, account names, and public payment instructions. | Required before donation-channel activation |

## Required before public launch

Confirm the organization profile and public contact policy. Existing content-ready states may remain visibly incomplete, but they must not imply approvals, people, locations, or payment availability that have not been granted.

## Required before feature activation

Activate a project detail, impact report, story, transparency download, map, or payment method only when its row’s inputs are current, approved, and publishable. Record the responsible reviewer and the approval date in the applicable operational process; do not place private verification material in the public repository.

## Optional enhancements

Approved report cover art, translated content, supplementary project media, video, and richer contextual material are optional only after the associated factual and consent requirements are complete.

## Operating rule

When an item is missing, leave the existing clear boundary in place. Do not substitute placeholder facts, anonymous testimonials, generic stock photography, or stale financial instructions.
